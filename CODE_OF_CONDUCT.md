# Community code of conduct

We want Commonplace to be a respectful, practical place to build together. This applies to issues, pull requests, reviews and other project spaces.

- Treat people with respect, regardless of experience, background or identity.
- Discuss specific behavior and code. Give actionable feedback and accept corrections.
- Credit others' work and respect privacy, intellectual property and consent.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, publish private information, spam or impersonate others.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, close disruptive discussions, and restrict participation when necessary. Depending on severity, this may begin with a warning or result in immediate removal. Apply these standards consistently, including to maintainers.

For public moderation issues, mention @saketh12e in the relevant discussion without repeating private or harmful content. Use GitHub's private **Report content** flow for sensitive reports; do not post a person's private information in an issue. Security vulnerabilities follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
