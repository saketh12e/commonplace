**Brief and evidence boundaries**

Commonplace is an open-source software directory for people discovering, comparing, and saving useful projects across devices. The requested direction is a light neutral professional product interface: genuine project logos beside names, restrained glass feedback on controls, no gradients or neon, and usable desktop, tablet, and phone layouts.

The Refero design skill supplied the research method. Styles were searched before screens and flows.

Evidence labels throughout:

- **Full style:** Refero's semantic extraction of a captured page. These are actionable reference notes, not a verified live DOM or an official brand specification.
- **Screen metadata:** full Refero descriptions, layout, and function notes. Some function notes infer behavior that a static image cannot prove.
- **Visually inspected:** the returned image was displayed and examined in this task.
- **Adaptation:** a proposed Commonplace decision derived from evidence and the brief; not a claim that it already exists or was tested.

Refero captures may describe earlier versions of products. The lookup is live; capture freshness was not established. No existing Commonplace screen was audited during this research-only task.

**Three full onboarding flows**

These were retrieved with `refero_get_flow`, including per-step goals, actions, system-response descriptions, and screen IDs. No onboarding interaction was executed.

1. **VSCO — flow 422, Account Creation and Onboarding:** [full Refero flow](https://refero.design/flows/422). The complete record includes account creation, an inline signup error, a skippable welcome, role selection, interests, and a feed. Relevant steps are [welcome](https://refero.design/pages/88fbffa2-754d-4bc5-a8cf-1dfa84de27b1), [role selection](https://refero.design/pages/577c2e76-bd25-41f8-a312-cab0cb54d702), and [interests](https://refero.design/pages/9193a0bf-b262-4051-8668-92a1c2252329). **Adapt:** clear selected states and a visible Skip route. The short personalization segment is useful; the complete signup journey is not a proposed Commonplace requirement.
2. **Cohere — flow 3790, Account registration and onboarding:** [full Refero flow](https://refero.design/flows/3790). After registration, name, role, and intended use form a numbered sequence. [Role screen](https://refero.design/pages/b327875f-b0cb-4343-9a37-903ebfb6688e) includes Back and Skip; [use-case screen](https://refero.design/pages/2378f9ee-19f9-44c0-bbaf-132d3bcb29c9) supports multiple selections and custom input. **Adapt:** explain single versus multiple selection, show progress, and make answers editable. Omit the pastel decorative background and mandatory identity collection.
3. **Clay — flow 5853, Account creation and first-run onboarding:** [full Refero flow](https://refero.design/flows/5853). The [choose-platform step](https://refero.design/pages/a5e3b655-6cdb-4f34-9f50-cc98fbc967e3) offers desktop/mobile/web entry points, followed by preferences and a longer product tour. **Adapt:** recognizable platform choices and a clear continue-in-browser path. Clay demonstrates client choice; it does **not** establish a multi-select software-compatibility filter. Commonplace's macOS/Windows/Linux/web preference is a proposed adaptation, reinforced by Raycast's catalog platform controls. Omit Clay's acquisition survey and eight-card tour.

Proposed Commonplace sequence: catalog is immediately available → optional “Tailor your discoveries” → choose work interests/role → choose platforms → Show matching projects. Skip returns to the catalog; Back retains selections; preferences remain editable beside catalog filters. These states and the two-step length are Commonplace design proposals. Role can guide suggestions; explicit platform filters should remain visible so users understand why results changed.

**Reference lock and decision ledger**

Primary foundation: **Apple's neutral light surface hierarchy.** Preserve white/near-white fields, graphite text, precise type hierarchy, discreet pill controls, and genuine product imagery. Secondary contributions: Linear's compact typography/spacing and Shop's prominent search with contained item imagery. Screens supply product structure; flows supply optional personalization sequencing.

| Decision                 | Source and role                                                  | Proposed Commonplace commitment                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| ------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Canvas and surfaces      | Apple full style: base/elevated/grouped layers                   | Canvas `#f5f5f7`; content `#ffffff`; grouped controls may use `#e2e2e5`. Avoid decorative background effects.                                                                                                                                                 |
| Text hierarchy           | Apple neutral roles; Linear type discipline                      | Primary `#1d1d1f`, secondary `#333333`; reserve `#707070` for less prominent metadata after contrast checks. Use a coherent sans-serif stack; Inter is the Linear-derived option. No ornamental serif word swaps.                                             |
| Density                  | Linear compact component rhythm                                  | Start with 8px related-item gaps, 16px entry padding, and 24px group gaps. Use 14–16px readable UI copy and moderate heading weights. These are candidate design values, not measured Commonplace output.                                                     |
| Actions and focus        | Apple interaction roles; user neutral brief                      | Keep most utility controls neutral. If blue is used, confine `#0071e3` to decisive actions/focus and `#0066cc` to links. Do not use brand colors as page washes.                                                                                              |
| Logos beside names       | Raycast inspected catalog; explicit user requirement             | Authentic project mark in a fixed 32–40px slot immediately before its name at every viewport; preserve aspect ratio. Verify each asset's source during implementation. Do not invent a brand mark when absent.                                                |
| Glass hover shift        | User requirement; Apple Books static glass; bundled motion guide | Propose a 120ms neutral fill/border change with at most 1px vertical movement on eligible controls. Use a restrained inset highlight and subtle shadow; keep text opaque. The movement and exact glass recipe are adaptations, not Refero-measured animation. |
| Input and sheet behavior | Bundled craft; Apple Books sheet                                 | Visible keyboard focus, named controls, stable image dimensions, and clear selected states. Keep hover optional for touch. Reduced motion removes translation; opaque fallback preserves clarity.                                                             |
| Search and filtering     | Raycast, Shop, Target                                            | Search + platform + category + sort near the catalog; show count and applied filters. Preserve query/filter state on details/back navigation; this persistence is a craft-led requirement to test later.                                                      |
| Shelf organization       | Apple Books picker/library                                       | Separate Save control, selectable shelves with counts, explicit creation action, and a clear saved confirmation.                                                                                                                                              |
| Personalization          | VSCO, Cohere, Clay                                               | Optional two-step role/interests and platform setup with Skip, Back, and editable preferences. Browsing must remain useful without setup.                                                                                                                     |

Bundled Refero motion and craft guidance informed these proposed interaction rules. Motion serves feedback, continuity and hierarchy. Focus, touch behavior, reduced motion, dimensions and URL state are implementation requirements to validate.
